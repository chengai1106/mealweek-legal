What is free

The app is free to download and the full planning flow is free to use. The free tier includes a fixed number of dinners from the recipe library. The exact number is shown on the first screen and on the purchase screen, before you are asked to pay for anything.

Subscriptions

  • Weekly — US$4.99 per week
  • Monthly — US$9.99 per month
  • Yearly — US$49.99 per year

Prices shown are for the United States App Store and may differ elsewhere. Every plan shows its annualised cost on the purchase screen so you can compare them directly.

A subscription unlocks the complete dinner library and the new recipes added in monthly app updates.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings after purchase.

Subscriptions support Apple Family Sharing.

If your subscription ends

Locked recipes become unavailable, but the meal plans and shopping lists you already saved remain readable. Your own data is not held hostage.

Recipes and nutrition

Recipes are provided for general home cooking. They are not medical or dietary advice. If you have a food allergy or a medical condition, always check ingredient labels yourself. The app's exclusion settings filter the recipe library, but they cannot verify what is in the food you actually buy.
