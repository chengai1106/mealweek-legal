Support

Usually answered within two business days. Email xiangmucheng001@gmail.com. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version makes a fix much faster.

I set an ingredient to never show, but it still appeared

This should never happen, and we want to hear about it. Send us the ingredient you typed and the name of the dinner it appeared in. Exclusions match ingredient names, so an unusual spelling can slip through, and that is a bug worth fixing.

I paid but the full library is still locked

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, email us.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Planning, swapping dinners, generating the shopping list, and checking items off all work in airplane mode. Subscription options, purchases, restores, and entitlement checks use a connection.

How do I cancel?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancelling in the App Store, not inside our app, is how Apple handles all subscriptions.

Will I lose my meal plans if I stop paying?

No. Plans and shopping lists you already saved stay readable after a subscription ends.

Can my family use one subscription?

Yes. Subscriptions support Apple Family Sharing.
