Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 August 2026

The app does not collect, sell, share, or transmit your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is written to your iPhone's local storage and never leaves it:

  • Your weekly dinner plans, past and present
  • Your shopping lists and which items you have checked off
  • Your preferences: servings, ingredients you never want to see, eating style, dinners per week, and time limit

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is no cloud copy, because there is no server.

The one thing that does leave your device

If you buy a subscription, the purchase itself is handled by Apple, and subscription status is verified through RevenueCat, our purchase infrastructure provider.

RevenueCat receives purchase history only, to confirm whether your subscription is active. Per RevenueCat's own privacy manifest, this data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. Its declared purpose is app functionality.

We never see your name, email, or payment details. Apple does not share them with us.

If you never make a purchase, nothing leaves your device at all.

Permissions we do not ask for

The app requests no system permissions. It does not access your location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts, calendar, health data, or notifications. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not present App Tracking Transparency prompts, because it does not track you.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and is safe for family use. It does not contain user-generated content, chat, links to external websites inside the app, or an in-app browser. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your data after a subscription ends

If your subscription lapses, the meal plans and shopping lists you already saved remain readable. We do not lock you out of your own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
