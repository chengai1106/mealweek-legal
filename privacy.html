Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Your meal plans, shopping lists, and food preferences stay on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. Subscription infrastructure may contact Apple and RevenueCat to load subscription options and verify purchase entitlement.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is written to your iPhone's local storage and never leaves it:

  • Your weekly dinner plans, past and present
  • Your shopping lists and which items you have checked off
  • Your preferences: servings, ingredients you never want to see, eating style, dinners per week, and time limit

Deleting the app deletes this data. There is no cloud copy of your plans, lists, or preferences; the app has no account or content backend.

Subscription services

The app may contact Apple and RevenueCat, our purchase infrastructure provider, to load subscription options and check purchase entitlement. If you buy or restore a subscription, Apple handles the transaction and RevenueCat verifies subscription status.

RevenueCat's Apple privacy manifest declares Purchase History for app functionality. That declared data type is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

We never see your name, email, or payment details. Apple does not share them with us.

Your meal plans, shopping lists, and food preferences are not sent to RevenueCat or Apple. Subscription-related requests may still occur before a purchase so the app can load available plans and check entitlement status.

Permissions we do not ask for

The app requests no system permissions. It does not access your location, camera, photos, microphone, contacts, calendar, health data, or notifications. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not present App Tracking Transparency prompts, because it does not track you.

Children

The app is rated 4+. It does not contain user-generated content, chat, or an in-app browser. Terms, Privacy, and Support links open external pages in your browser. We do not ask children or other users to create an account or provide profile information.

Your data after a subscription ends

If your subscription lapses, the meal plans and shopping lists you already saved remain readable. We do not lock you out of your own data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
